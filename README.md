Hi there! I work on tools to write more powerful, legible, and ergonomic software specifications, and therefore more correct software writ large.

I work at [Theorem](https://theorem.dev/), where I earnestly believe we have a shot at formally verifying all software.

Previously, at [Antithesis](https://antithesis.com), I co-created [Hegel](https://hegel.dev/), a universal property-based testing library.

I maintain [Hypothesis](https://github.com/hypothesisWorks/hypothesis/), Python's property-based testing library. I [built its new core representation](https://github.com/HypothesisWorks/hypothesis/issues/3921#issuecomment-2823204378) and [made it thread-safe](https://hypothesis.works/articles/thread-safe/). I also worked on [HypoFuzz](https://hypofuzz.com/) together with [Zac](https://zhd.dev/).

As for academia: I co-authored [this NeurIPS workshop paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09907) in October 2025 [with Anthropic](https://red.anthropic.com/2026/property-based-testing/). I released [the Hypothesis Corpus](https://hypothesis.works/articles/hypothesis-corpus/) in April 2026, the largest ever PBT dataset. And I was a participant in [both](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/) [iterations](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/) of [METR](https://metr.org/)'s AI productivity study.
